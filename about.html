Our Mission

Bringing DIY Knowledge Back To Communities

ToolTutors was created to bridge the gap between watching online tutorials and having a skilled person standing beside you. We connect homeowners, learners, hobbyists, and professionals through a trusted local marketplace built around practical knowledge, mentorship, and real-world experience.

Why ToolTutors Exists

Millions of people want to improve their homes, learn practical skills, and complete projects themselves. Yet many discover that online videos cannot answer questions in real time, identify mistakes, or provide reassurance when something goes wrong.

On the other hand, hiring a professional tradesperson for every task can be expensive, especially when the goal is simply to learn a new skill rather than outsource the entire project.

ToolTutors was built around a simple idea: connect people who want to learn with people who already know. By doing so, we create opportunities for knowledge sharing, skill development, and stronger local communities.

How ToolTutors Works

A simple process from idea to completion.

1. Post Your Project

Describe your DIY project, upload photos, share your budget, and explain whether you want guidance or a completed solution.

2. Receive Offers

Qualified tutors in your area review your request and submit offers explaining pricing, experience, and availability.

3. Learn or Complete

Meet your chosen tutor, collaborate on the project, gain confidence, and leave a review once the work is complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need DIY experience?

No. ToolTutors is designed for complete beginners through to experienced enthusiasts.

Can anyone become a Tutor?

Tutors must complete the platform's review and verification process before marketplace access is granted.

What's the difference between Teach Me and Complete For Me?

Teach Me focuses on mentorship and learning, while Complete For Me is for professional completion of work.

Can I communicate with a Tutor before hiring them?

Yes. The platform includes messaging functionality and proposal workflows to help both parties coordinate.
