Safety Agreement & Liability Waiver

Please read and acknowledge our safety protocols before participating in mentorship sessions.

1. Assumption of Risk

Participants acknowledge that DIY activities involving power tools, construction materials, and hand tools carry inherent physical risks. Both tutors and learners agree to wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) at all times.

2. Platform Role

ToolTutors provides an introductory mentorship network platform. ToolTutors is not a licensed contracting firm and does not provide formal legal warranty over physical work executed by users. Tutors act as independent educational mentors.
