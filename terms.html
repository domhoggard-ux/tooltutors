1. Introduction and Platform Role

ToolTutors operates as a disclosed introductory and payment collection agent connecting individuals seeking practical DIY assistance ("Learners") with independent individuals offering guidance, mentoring, or contracted services ("Tutors").

ToolTutors does not employ Tutors and does not itself provide the underlying DIY mentoring or contracted service. When a Learner accepts a Tutor's offer, the agreement for the underlying service is formed directly between the Learner and the Tutor.

The Tutor remains the supplier of the underlying service. ToolTutors acts as the Tutor's disclosed agent solely for introducing the parties, providing the marketplace and communication facilities, arranging payment, collecting payment from the Learner, deducting the agreed platform commission, and remitting the remaining amount to the Tutor.

2. Teach Me Engagements

A "Teach Me" engagement is an educational and advisory service supplied by the Tutor directly to the Learner.

The Learner remains responsible for performing the physical work and for deciding whether it is safe and appropriate to proceed. The Tutor provides explanation, demonstration, mentoring, and practical guidance but is not contracted to complete the work on the Learner's behalf.

The Learner accepts responsibility for the work performed by the Learner, including any resulting damage, injury, regulatory issue, or unsuccessful outcome, except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded.

3. Complete For Me Engagements

A "Complete For Me" engagement is a contracted service supplied directly by an approved professional-tier Tutor to the Learner.

The Tutor acts as an independent contractor and not as an employee, worker, partner, or subcontractor of ToolTutors.

The Tutor is responsible for holding any qualification, registration, certification, insurance, or regulatory approval required for the work. The Tutor is also responsible for the quality, safety, legality, and completion of the service supplied to the Learner.

Any complaint or claim concerning workmanship, advice, damage, delay, injury, or regulatory compliance must be addressed to the Tutor who supplied the service, subject to any assistance or mediation ToolTutors voluntarily provides.

4. Payments and Agency Collection

The Learner authorises ToolTutors to collect payment as the disclosed payment collection agent of the selected Tutor.

Payment through ToolTutors satisfies the Learner's obligation to pay the Tutor for the amount successfully collected through the platform.

ToolTutors is authorised by the Tutor to deduct the agreed platform commission and any clearly disclosed adjustments before remitting the remaining balance to the Tutor through the connected Stripe account.

Unless a different rate has been expressly agreed, ToolTutors deducts a platform commission equal to 10% of the agreed job price. The remaining 90% is allocated to the Tutor's connected Stripe account.

Stripe or another authorised payment provider may process payments, transfers, refunds, disputes, and payouts. The timing of a bank payout may depend on the payment provider's verification, processing, reserve, and payout arrangements.

5. Invoices and Payment Records

The Tutor authorises ToolTutors and its payment provider to prepare and issue invoices, receipts, credit notes, and related payment documentation on the Tutor's behalf for services supplied by the Tutor to a Learner.

The Tutor is responsible for ensuring that the legal name, trading name, business address, contact details, VAT registration number, and other tax information held by ToolTutors or Stripe are accurate and current.

ToolTutors may provide the Tutor with a separate commission invoice, commission statement, or settlement record showing the gross job value, ToolTutors commission, adjustments, and the net amount allocated to the Tutor.

Where commission has already been deducted from money collected for the Tutor, the commission document may be marked "Paid by deduction" and no second payment will be due.

6. Tax and VAT Responsibilities

Each Tutor is responsible for their own income tax, National Insurance, VAT registration, VAT accounting, record keeping, and any other tax obligation arising from services supplied through ToolTutors.

A VAT-registered Tutor must provide an accurate VAT registration number and must notify ToolTutors promptly of any change in VAT status.

ToolTutors is responsible for tax and VAT arising from its own agency and platform commission. Until ToolTutors is VAT registered, VAT will not be added to the ToolTutors commission. If ToolTutors becomes VAT registered, VAT may be charged on the commission in accordance with applicable law and the Tutor will be notified of the change.

7. Cancellations and Refunds

Cancellation and refund eligibility may depend on the job status, the agreed appointment time, work already performed, materials purchased, and the circumstances of the cancellation.

Where a refund is approved, ToolTutors may arrange it through Stripe on behalf of the Tutor. Any related reversal of Tutor funds or commission will be recorded against the relevant transaction.

8. Disputes

Learners and Tutors should first attempt to resolve service-related concerns directly through the ToolTutors communication system.

ToolTutors may review platform records, assist communication, suspend an account, or provide informal mediation. ToolTutors does not become the supplier of the underlying service by providing that assistance.

ToolTutors may retain records reasonably required to operate the marketplace, administer payments, investigate disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and maintain accounting records.
