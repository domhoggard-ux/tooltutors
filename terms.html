1. Introduction and Platform Role

Welcome to ToolTutors. ToolTutors operates strictly as an introductory platform connecting individuals seeking DIY assistance ("Learners") with individuals offering guidance or contracting services ("Tutors").

ToolTutors is not a contracting agency, an employer, or a trades service provider. We do not employ Tutors, nor do we guarantee the outcome, safety, or quality of the engagements formed through the platform. By using ToolTutors, you acknowledge that any agreement formed is solely between the Learner and the Tutor.

2. "Teach Me" Engagements (Peer Mentorship)

If a Learner selects a "Teach Me" engagement, the service provided by the Tutor is strictly educational and advisory (peer mentorship).

  • Learner Liability: The Learner remains the primary actor and is physically responsible for performing the work on their own property. The Learner assumes 100% of the risk associated with the task.
  • Tutor Role: The Tutor is present to advise, instruct, and supervise. The Tutor is not contracted to perform physical labour.
  • Waiver of Claims: The Learner agrees to hold both ToolTutors and the attending Tutor entirely harmless from any property damage, physical injury, or code compliance failures resulting from a "Teach Me" session.

3. "Complete For Me" Engagements (Contracted Services)

If a Learner selects a "Complete For Me" engagement, the service is limited exclusively to Tutors verified as "Professionals" by the platform.

  • Independent Contractor Status: The Professional Tutor operates as an independent contractor, not an employee of ToolTutors.
  • Insurance and Liability: The Professional Tutor is solely responsible for maintaining appropriate public liability insurance. Any claims regarding defective workmanship, property damage, or injury must be directed entirely to the Professional Tutor, not ToolTutors.
  • Regulatory Compliance: The Professional Tutor warrants that they hold the necessary licenses or certifications required by UK law to perform the requested tradeswork (e.g., Part P for electrical, Gas Safe for plumbing/heating).

4. Payments and Cancellations

All payments processed through the platform are subject to our secure holding policy. Funds are held in escrow and released to the Tutor upon the Learner marking the job as "Completed."

If a Tutor cancels an engagement prior to the scheduled time, the job will automatically be returned to the open marketplace. If a Learner cancels within 24 hours of a scheduled appointment, a cancellation fee may apply.

In the event of a dispute regarding the quality of work or advice, both parties agree to attempt amicable resolution directly. ToolTutors may, at its sole discretion, offer mediation services or investigate breaches of platform conduct, but is not legally obligated to enforce restitution.
