Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

ToolTutors is the trading name responsible for the ToolTutors platform. Our contact address is 24 The Fairway, Darlington, DL1 1ES. Privacy enquiries can be sent to Admin@Tooltutors.co.uk.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how the platform is used, we may collect account and contact information, profile photographs, addresses and location coordinates, job requests, messages, reviews, offers, appointment details, payment and transfer references, invoices, support records and account activity.

For Tutors, we may also collect evidence submitted for account review, including DBS certificates, trade or professional documents, skills, review decisions and administrative notes. Stripe collects and manages identity, business and payout information during connected-account onboarding. ToolTutors may receive selected account status and contact information from Stripe, but does not receive or store full payment-card details.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to create and secure accounts, connect Learners and Tutors, operate messaging and job workflows, process and reconcile payments, support users, investigate disputes, prevent misuse, administer invoices, review Tutor identity and evidence, maintain platform records and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Our lawful bases

Depending on the activity, ToolTutors processes personal information because it is necessary to perform or prepare a contract, comply with a legal obligation, or pursue legitimate interests such as operating and securing the marketplace, preventing fraud, supporting users and reviewing account suitability. Where consent is required for a specific activity, such as a permitted DBS Update Service status check, consent will be requested separately.

Information relating to criminal convictions and offences receives additional legal protection. ToolTutors will process such information only where a lawful basis and an applicable condition under UK data-protection law have been identified and documented.

5. Tutor identity, DBS and evidence information

“Identity and evidence reviewed” means that ToolTutors has reviewed information and documents submitted for a Tutor account. It is not a guarantee of future conduct, an endorsement for every type of project, or a statement that every document remains current indefinitely.

A DBS certificate upload is treated as restricted account-review information. Access is limited to authorised administration functions. Where ToolTutors is legally entitled to conduct a DBS Update Service status check, ToolTutors will obtain the Tutor's permission and follow the applicable requirements. ToolTutors may record the date, checker and outcome of a status check, rather than reproducing unnecessary criminal-record details.

6. Who we share information with

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, authentication, storage, email, payment processing, fraud prevention and platform operations. Stripe processes payment and connected-account information under its own privacy terms. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the platform, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Private addresses and other restricted information are not intended for public display. Relevant job information may be shared between a matched Learner and Tutor when needed to deliver an engagement.

7. Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including account operation, safeguarding, dispute handling, fraud prevention, financial record keeping and legal compliance. Evidence that is no longer required should be securely deleted or access-restricted. Different records may have different retention periods.

8. Security

We use access controls, authenticated accounts, restricted storage and secure service providers to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their sign-in details and notify ToolTutors promptly about suspected account misuse.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to certain processing, request portability and withdraw consent where consent is the basis used. A request can be sent to Admin@Tooltutors.co.uk. Identity may need to be confirmed before a request is completed.

Individuals also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when platform features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will show its effective date at the top of this page.
